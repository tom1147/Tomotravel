Frequently asked questions

What happens if I don’t sign up for website care?

Your site simply stays online. If you need changes later, just ask — I’ll quote each fix individually.

Will my site go offline if I cancel the care plan?

No. The site is yours, so it keeps running after you cancel.

Can I hire you if I don’t have text or photos?

Yes. Tell me about your business or project and I’ll write the copy. If you don’t have photos, I’ll finish the site with suitable stock or illustrative images, which you can swap for your own later.

I don’t have a domain (a web address like yourname.com).

You can launch on a free address (for example, your-shop.pages.dev) at no cost. That said, I don’t really recommend staying on a free address: for a shop or business, your own domain is easier to remember and builds trust. If you’d like one, I’ll email you simple steps to get it (the domain fee is paid by you).

When and how do I pay?

Small projects are usually paid upfront. I’ll share payment options when we discuss your project.

Can I hire you from outside Japan?

Absolutely. We work mostly by email, so I can take on projects from anywhere in the world.
