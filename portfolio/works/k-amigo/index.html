K.AMIGO

The official site of K.AMIGO KTV BAR, a Japanese-style KTV bar in Malate, Manila. It’s structured so Japanese visitors can check everything they want to know before visiting — prices, menus, location and how to book — on a single page.

Design highlights

  • Neon nights as the brand

    Inspired by Manila’s neon nightlife, a dark design lets the pink glow capture the venue’s atmosphere.

  • Japanese headlines take center stage

    Large Japanese headlines lead the page, with English used as an atmospheric accent.

  • Photos and video show the venue

    A photo gallery and an introduction video let first-time guests see inside before they visit.
