KISS ME

The brand site for KISS ME, a Japanese-style KTV bar in Ermita, near Malate in Manila. Brush-script lettering, Polaroid-style photos and neon signs come together to bring the personality of the name to life on the web.

Design highlights

  • The name becomes the visual

    A large brush-script “KISS ME” at the top tells visitors the name and character of the place at a glance.

  • A collage-style layout

    Slightly tilted, overlapping Polaroid-style photos and cards make the page playful rather than overly neat.

  • A palette built around pink

    The logo’s pink accents the whole site, so the brand color stands out even among night photos.
